01 · CAPACITY

EdgePort + Echo

Passed

7 min · SIPp 3.7.3, UDP

What it measures

The EdgePort (Java) on its own, with Echo answering every request with 200 OK. No Dispatcher, Connect or database, so this is a ceiling for EdgePort alone, not for a full deployment.

Why it matters

The EdgePort is the only stateful piece of Routr. When it saturates, calls fail, no matter what runs behind it.

How it runs

Calibration run: SIPp sends SIP MESSAGE requests over UDP, starting at 500 messages/s and adding 100 every 30 s, up to 3,000 messages/s. The first 20 s warm the EdgePort up and are not measured. The run stops at the first step where p99 MESSAGE-to-200 latency reaches 50 ms or failed requests reach 0.1%.

RoutrOne, 1 hour

What it measures

The whole stack in one image, EdgePort plus Dispatcher, Connect, Location and the data service in one Node process, routing calls between registered agents.

Why it matters

Leaks only show after hours. A short test can pass while memory climbs until the process is killed in production.

How it runs

Calibration run: SIPp holds a steady 5 calls/s for 1 hour, with 200 registered callee agents, each called by its own caller agent through Connect. After a 15-minute warm-up, each process's memory may grow at most 5 MB/h.
